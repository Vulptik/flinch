"Flinch", the Flinch logo and app icon, and "by Vulptik" are trademarks of Vulptik. The code
licenses (GPL-3.0-only for the app, MIT for the installer) cover the code. They do not grant any
right to these trademarks.

The rules below let people use and share the code freely while making sure nobody mistakes a
fork for the official app.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying truthfully that your project is based on or forked from Flinch, for example
  "MyCapture, a fork of Flinch".
- Writing about, reviewing, teaching or linking to Flinch by name.
- Sharing unmodified official builds from the Microsoft Store, https://flinch.vulptik.dev or this
  repository's GitHub Releases, under their original name.
- Building and running modified versions for your own private use.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name Flinch or a confusingly similar name.
- Using the Flinch logo or app icon, or a confusingly similar one, in a modified version, its
  installer, Store listing, website or promotion.
- Publishing any build, modified or not, to the Microsoft Store or another app store under the
  Flinch name or logo.
- Using Flinch in a product name, company name, domain name or social media account.
- Suggesting that Vulptik made, endorses or supports your version.

## If you fork Flinch

Before distributing a fork, give it a new name and icon, replace `resources/store-identity.json`
and the package identity in `appxmanifest.xml`, and point update checks and support links to your
own project. The GPL-3.0 still requires you to keep the copyright notices and license, and to
offer your source code.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue on the Flinch repository.
